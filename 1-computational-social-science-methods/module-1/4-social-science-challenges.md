### Social Science Challenges

- Social science is able to handle simple and large systems using statistics or big data, but it breaks down on realistic, complex, _human_ systems, because of unpredictable, non-linear human interactions and a lack of reliable data.

- Sitting at the peak of the pyramid of all the sciences, social science is the complex and difficult of all sciences.

- Social science is pretty bad at producing reliable and reproducible results.

Perhaps the greatest challenge of social science is that there is no right way to go about it. David Hilbert, one of the most influential mathematicians of our time, asked the Entscheidungsproblem; he asked if there was a way to automate the decision if something is true or false. Just a few years later, Kurt Gödel, one of the most influential logicians of our time, came along with his Incompleteness Theorem, and proved that it is not possible to find a consistent system with only true statements.
