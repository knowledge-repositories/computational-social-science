### Fighting Poverty with Data

> Prof. Joshua Blumenstock, a professor of economics at the University of California, Berkeley, is involved in data-intensive international development research, or as he calls it, "fighting poverty with data".

Some of the key problems that are looked at include:

- Predicting poverty
- Crisis response
- Internal displacement
- Rural connectivity
- Community cohesion
- Financial inclusion

Despite big data being the enabler of emprical social science research, it can be considered somewhat of a "first world problem"; data is not collected as extensively and as effectively in developing countries.
